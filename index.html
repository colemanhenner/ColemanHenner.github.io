Research Projects

Worldwide Image Geolocalization Using Hierarchical Transfer Learning

Geolocalization from a single image is the process of estimating where that image was taken on Earth. This research introduces a novel transfer learning approach to a landmark paper on Geolocalization. As first autor, I've been delighted reach a nearly 30% improvement in mean error between an image's ground truth and predicted location compared with the original paper.
